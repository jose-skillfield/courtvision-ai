Your personal shooting coach, in your pocket.

Point your phone at the court. CourtVision AI tracks your shooting motion, arc, release speed and ball flight in real time, then talks you through every rep.

Everything runs on your device. No video leaves your phone.

Real-time audio feedback

Instant spoken cues through your earbuds, like "Arc 42°. Add power" or "Higher release", so you can train alone.

Shot charting & quality score

Makes and misses are tagged automatically, and each shot gets a 0–100 quality score with a difficulty rating based on depth and balance.

Form breakdown

Step through each shot frame by frame to check elbow tuck, dip consistency and follow-through on your release.

Set up in 30 seconds

  1. Side-on view. Prop your phone 4–6 m from your shooting spot, side-on, with your whole body and the rim in frame.
  2. Tap the rim. Tap the hoop in the preview once so we can tell makes from misses.
  3. Earbuds in. Connect earbuds for spoken cues, then shoot.
